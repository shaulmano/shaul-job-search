LINKEDIN OUTREACH - two messages, used in this order.

===============================================================
STEP 1: CONNECTION REQUEST NOTE
LinkedIn caps this at 300 characters. This one is 247, so you have
a little room to add a personal line if you know something about them.
===============================================================

Hi {first_name}, I'm a QA leader with 25 years behind me, most recently Head of QA
at Qmarkets. I'm looking for my next role and hoping one of your portfolio companies
needs someone to run quality, especially with AI in the product. Would be glad to
connect.


===============================================================
STEP 2: SEND THIS ONCE THEY ACCEPT
Wait for the accept. Do not paste this into the connection request.
===============================================================

Thanks for connecting, {first_name}.

Quick context. I'm Shaul Mano, 25 years in software quality. The last 15 running QA
for entire product areas and managing global teams: Head of QA at Qmarkets until
January this year, and before that RSA Security, Symantec and Promisec. At Qmarkets
I brought post-release production bugs down by 35%.

This past year I've been building and testing AI agents myself, and working out how
you test software that doesn't give you the same answer twice. Plenty of teams are
shipping AI features right now with no good way to answer "is this actually good
enough to release?" That's the part I'm good at.

So if one of your portfolio companies is growing its R&D team and needs someone to
run quality, I'd really appreciate an introduction. Happy to send my CV over, or to
talk to any founder for 15 minutes.

Thanks,
Shaul


===============================================================
WHO TO SEND IT TO
===============================================================

On the fund's LinkedIn company page, click "People", then type one of these into
the search box on that tab:

    talent        platform        people        HR        recruit

You want a title like Head of Talent, Talent Partner, VP Talent, Head of Platform,
or People Partner. That person's whole job is connecting people to portfolio
companies, so this is a normal request for them, not an imposition.

If the fund is small and has nobody like that, go to a Partner instead. Smaller
funds handle talent at partner level.
